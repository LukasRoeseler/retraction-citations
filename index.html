About this tool

This dashboard combines two open datasets:

  • The Retraction Watch database (maintained by Crossref) — the most comprehensive list of retracted scholarly papers.
  • OpenCitations — open scholarly citation data.

For every retraction in the database that has a usable DOI for the original paper, we pull all incoming citations from OpenCitations (once, cached) and tally them by year. The aggregate event-study aligns each paper at the year it was retracted. The browse table lets you drill into any specific retraction.

Built in the spirit of the FLoRA Citation Impact Explorer.
