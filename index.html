About this tool

This dashboard combines two open datasets:

  • The Retraction Watch database (maintained by Crossref) — the most comprehensive catalogue of retracted scholarly papers.
  • OpenCitations — open scholarly citation data.

For every retraction with a usable DOI, we pull incoming citations from OpenCitations and tally them by year. The aggregate event-study in the Citations tab aligns each paper at the year it was retracted and estimates the average change in citation volume using a two-way fixed-effects model. Data are refreshed monthly.

The Trends tab shows retraction counts by year, most common retraction reasons, and top journals and authors by number of retractions.

Data sources

  • Retraction Watch database — Crossref / Retraction Watch. Used under their open data terms.
  • OpenCitations Index v2 API — open citation data released under CC0.

Methodology notes

  • Retractions whose original-paper DOI begins with xx (Retraction Watch convention for unknown DOIs) are excluded.
  • Citation counts come from OpenCitations COCI; coverage varies by publisher and year.
  • The event-study model uses log(1 + citations) as the outcome, with study and calendar-year fixed effects and standard errors clustered at the study level.
  • The pre-retraction trend in the model is not flat, which likely reflects the systematic selection of highly-cited papers for retraction. Estimates should be interpreted descriptively.
